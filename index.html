GRAND PARAGON

Paragon Restaurant The Paragon Restaurant in Kozhikode (Calicut) is a legendary, historic restaurant and culinary landmark rather than a lodging hotel Founded: 1939 by Govindan Panhikeyil in Calicut. • Leadership: Headed by Chairman and CEO Sumesh Govind. • Cuisine: Renowned for authentic Malabar, Moplah, and coastal Kerala seafood traditions. • Recognition: Ranked globally among the world most iconic legacy restaurants by culinary platforms like TasteAtlas.
