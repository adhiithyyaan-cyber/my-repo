lThe Paragon Restaurant in Kozhikode (Calicut) is a legendary, historic restaurant and culinary landmark rather than a lodging hotel.

Location & Hours

• Address: Kannur Road, Near CH Flyover, Calicut Fort Gate, Kozhikode, Kerala 673001, India
• Opening Hours: 6:00 AM – 11:30 PM (Daily)
• Phone: +91 495 2767020 / +91 98464 97611

History & Overview

• Founded: 1939 by Govindan Panhikeyil in Calicut.
• Leadership: Headed by Chairman and CEO Sumesh Govind.
• Cuisine: Renowned for authentic Malabar, Moplah, and coastal Kerala seafood traditions.
• Recognition: Ranked globally among the world’s most iconic legacy restaurants by culinary platforms like TasteAtlas.
• Other Branches: Extended to locations including Kochi (Edappally and Lulu Mall) and Dubai in the UAE.

Signature Dishes

• Malabar Chicken & Mutton Biriyani
• Seafood Specialties: Tawa Prawns, Fish Mango Curry, Karimeen Polichattu, and Prawn Mango Curry
• Local Favorites: Beef Varattiyathu, Appam, Kerala Parotta, and Tender Coconut Pudding (Elaneer Pudding)
